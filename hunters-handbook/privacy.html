Aussie Hunter's Handbook: privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It works fully offline.

Updates: the app only goes online when you press "Get updates". It then downloads one content file (rules, tips and answers) from GitHub Pages. It sends nothing about you; like any website, GitHub may see your internet address in its normal server logs.

Location: if you tap "Find me" or "Save vehicle spot", the app asks for your location to show your coordinates and the distance back to your vehicle. Your location stays on your phone and is never shared or sent.

Saved items: favourites, your saved vehicle spot, rifle cards and your trip log are stored only on your phone. Share only sends them where you choose. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Links: official government pages open in your phone's own web browser, outside the app.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or notifications.
