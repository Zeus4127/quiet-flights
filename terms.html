What the app is

Quiet Flights gives estimates of how full flights usually are, based on past averages. It is a planning guide only. It does not sell tickets, check live seat availability, or know any airline's prices or upgrade rules.

No guarantees

Past patterns do not guarantee future ones. A month or week marked quiet may still be full, a suggested upgrade bid may lose, and fares and upgrade rules change. Always check with the airline before you book or bid. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for any booking, bid, cost or loss that follows from using the app. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Where the numbers come from

Flight and seat statistics, all adapted by Quiet Flights (the monthly averages and rhythms are our own work):

  • Australia: Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE), CC BY 4.0.
  • USA: U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics, T-100 Segment data (public domain).
  • Europe: Eurostat, avia_par. Eurostat is not responsible for the figures derived from it.
  • Brazil: Agência Nacional de Aviação Civil (ANAC), Dados Estatísticos do Transporte Aéreo, licensed CC BY.
  • Japan: 出典：「航空輸送統計調査」（国土交通省）を加工して作成 (MLIT air transport statistics, via e-Stat).

None of these agencies endorses this app. School holiday dates come from Australian state and territory education departments' published term dates. Upgrade bid reports come from public reports by Executive Traveller and Australian Frequent Flyer forum members, summarised as amounts, routes and dates, plus your own log.

Airline names are used only to describe the data. Quiet Flights is not connected with, or endorsed by, any airline.
