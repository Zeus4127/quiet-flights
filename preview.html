Try the Quiet Flights preview

Quiet Flights shows the months when planes on your route are usually emptiest, so business class is more likely to have spare seats and upgrade bids have a better chance. This is an early preview: please try it and tell us what works and what doesn't.

Open it

The web app works on any phone, tablet or computer. Android users can also install the app.

Open the web app Android app (APK, 0.9 MB)

In this preview: about 2,900 routes in Australia, the USA, Europe, Japan and Brazil, with data up to September 2026. Europe now shows its quiet months too. These are monthly averages from official airline statistics, not live seat counts.

New: Business | Economy switch. The switch at the top of the app opens Economy: cheap fares from Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, Perth and Adelaide that are well below other dates on the same route, including possible mistake fares. In this preview the economy fares are SAMPLE (made up) until live prices are switched on, and they can't be booked. Open Economy.

Install guide

Android app (APK)

  1. Tap Android app (APK) above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway. The preview isn't on the Play Store yet, so this is expected.
  4. Open Quiet Flights from your home screen.

The store version will use a different signing key, so remove this preview before installing it.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the web app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android web app or computer

  1. Open the web app in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Phone: tap ⋮ then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Computer: click the install icon at the right of the address bar.

Good to know

  • Forecasts are averages from past years, refreshed monthly. They aren't live seat counts or prices.
  • Some routes have no forecast yet, mostly routes with no recent data. Japan's data runs to March 2024.
  • Upgrade-bid hints come from a small number of reports. Your own bid log stays on your device.
  • Economy deals are SAMPLE data in this preview. Once live, they are cached fares that can change quickly: check the price before you pay. We may earn a commission on bookings made through a link.
