Q&A

Questions people ask.

Does it show live seat availability?

No. It uses monthly averages from government records to show when planes are usually emptiest. That's when your odds of a cheap business fare, a reward seat or a winning upgrade bid are highest.

Which airlines does it cover?

All scheduled airlines on each route, because the government figures count every flight. Upgrade bid examples are from Qantas and Virgin Australia. Quiet Flights isn't connected with any airline.

How much can I trust a route's pattern?

Each route has a trust score from 0 to 100, worked out by testing the pattern on past years it hadn't seen. Busy, regular routes score highest.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Your starred routes and bid log stay on your phone.
