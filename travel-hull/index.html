      Questions

      The detail, for people who want it.

      How does it work with no signal?

      Country facts, phrases, checklists and rates ship inside the app. When you have signal it refreshes small data files, and every screen shows when it was last updated.

      How are the step dates worked out?

      From the day you leave: start the paperwork 8 weeks before, book the extras from 6 weeks before, then travel day, day 1 and the nights you're away. "You are here" marks the step for today.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. There's no subscription and no ads. Some deal links pay us a commission when you book, and they're always marked as paid links.

      Do I need an account?

      No. Your trip, favourites, notes and documents stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

      Where does the data come from?

      Official airline statistics, NASA weather data, OpenStreetMap, public holiday calendars and exchange-rate feeds. Free sources that allow this use. No scraping.

      What does "coming next" mean?

      Tools with a dashed outline are planned but not built yet. We never show them as ready.

      What can't it do?

      It can't check live seat maps or guarantee a price. Entry rules change, so every rule links to the official source.
