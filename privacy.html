Quiet Flights

Privacy policy

Quiet Flights · last updated 9 November 2026

Quiet Flights shows which months and weeks flights are usually emptiest, for routes in Australia, the USA, Europe, Japan and Brazil. It is built so that we do not need, and do not collect, any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app or on this website.

What stays on your device

Your starred routes and any upgrade bids you log in "My bids" are saved only on your phone or in your browser. We never receive them. If you press Share, the app hands a spreadsheet file to the app you choose (for example email); what happens next is up to you and that app.

When the app goes online

The app works offline. When it checks for new data it downloads one public file from this website. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log requests (for example your IP address and the time) for security and reliability; see GitHub's privacy statement. The home page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to send them. We do not receive or use any of these logs.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
