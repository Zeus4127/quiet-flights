Quiet Flights

Privacy policy

Quiet Flights · last updated 12 November 2026

Quiet Flights shows which months and weeks flights are usually emptiest, for routes in Australia, the USA, Europe, Japan and Brazil. It is built so that we do not need, and do not collect, any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing reaches us. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app or on this website.

What stays on your device

Your starred routes, the upgrade bids you log in "My bids", your savings tally and everything in Trips (bookings you paste, share or scan, your day plans and saved trip packs) are saved only on your phone or in your browser. We never receive them. Screenshots you scan are read on your phone; the picture is not uploaded. The app never reads your email or text messages by itself: it only sees what you choose to paste or share into it. If you press Share or Download, the app hands a file to the app you choose; what happens next is up to you and that app.

When the app goes online

Most of the app works offline. It goes online only in these cases:

  • New flight data: it downloads one public file from this website. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log requests (for example your IP address and the time) for security and reliability; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Trip packs (only when you tap Save pack): the app sends the destination city name to Wikivoyage (Wikimedia Foundation) to fetch the travel guide, and sends your hotel's name and address (or the city name if you have no hotel) to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find it on the map, then asks an OpenStreetMap Overpass server for places nearby. Your bookings, booking codes, names and dates are never sent. These services may log your IP address under their own privacy policies.
  • Screenshot reading (only the first time you scan): the app downloads the free Tesseract reader from the jsDelivr network. After that it works offline, and your pictures stay on your phone.
  • Fonts: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to send them.
  • Links you tap (for example Smartraveller, maps or Google Flights) open those websites under their own policies.

We do not receive or use any of these logs.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
